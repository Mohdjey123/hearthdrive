Documents, and scanning paper with your phone.

**Documents**

- **A Documents page** lists your PDFs, Office files and photos of paperwork, newest first, with a picture of each first page.
- **Date, sender and amount.** HearthDrive reads them from each document's text. Open a document to correct them, or to add a title and a note; leave a field empty to go back to what was read. The file itself is never changed.
- **Search and filters.** Search goes through the text of every document. Filter by type (invoice, receipt, contract, utility bill and more), by year and by sender.
- **PDF previews.** PDFs now have thumbnails on Windows.

**Scanning (Android)**

- **Scan** on the Documents page opens Google's document scanner: it finds the page edges, straightens and cleans up each page, and makes a PDF.
- The first scan asks which computer and folder to keep scans in. Later scans go there by themselves, and HearthDrive reads them like any other document. The scanner needs Google Play services on the phone.

Update the computer that holds your documents: the Documents page shows that computer's documents, and the reading runs there. Computers install updates by themselves once their HearthDrive window is closed.
