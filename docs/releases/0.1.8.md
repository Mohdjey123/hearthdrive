HearthDrive keeps running when you close its window, and it is now open source.

**Keeps running**

- **Windows.** Closing the window now hides HearthDrive in the notification area instead of quitting. Click its icon to bring the window back, or right-click it to quit. Opening HearthDrive again while it is running shows the window you already have.
- **Android.** Your phone stays connected to your other devices after you leave the app. It shows an ongoing HearthDrive notification, which has a Quit button. The first time, the app asks to show notifications.

**New look on Windows**

- The window has its own title bar in HearthDrive's colours instead of the standard Windows frame, with the usual minimise, maximise and close buttons.

**Open source**

- HearthDrive is now released under the Apache License 2.0. Settings shows the right license.

There's also a new website: https://mohdjey123.github.io/hearthdrive/

Coming from 0.1.4 or earlier? Install this by hand on one computer: it then passes the update on to all your other devices. From 0.1.5 on, every device finds updates here by itself.
