Updates from GitHub, family spaces, upload links, and much more you can do with your files.

**New**

- **Updates from anywhere.** Every device now also looks for updates here on GitHub, so it updates even when none of your other devices is reachable. Turn it off in **Settings → Updates**.
- **Android updates by itself.** On Android 12 and later, an update installs on its own the next time NEXUS is in the background (the first time, allow NEXUS to install apps).
- **Beta versions.** **Settings → Updates → Get beta versions** gets new versions before everyone else.
- **Groups and family spaces.** Put people together in a group and share folders with all of them at once; **New family space** makes a shared folder for a group in one step.
- **Upload links.** **Ask for files** on a folder gives a page where people without NEXUS can add files from your local network. They see nothing already there, and nothing is replaced.
- **Photos.** A timeline of every picture and video, a map, **On this day**, albums, and photos that look alike.
- **Music and films.** Music by artist and album, films and series, played in the app; a film started on one device continues on another.
- **Documents.** Search finds PDFs, Office files and scans by what they say, documents get tags (invoice, receipt, contract…), and PDFs open in the app.
- **Rules and suggestions.** Rules that tag, move, copy or clean up files by themselves, with a preview first, and suggestions when storage fills up.
- **File tools.** Duplicates, zip and 7z files that open like folders, downloads from a link, folders encrypted with a password, and plugins.
- **Programs, WebDAV and S3.** Map your storage as a network drive, or use it from rclone and backup programs, with access tokens.
- **Power and notifications.** Wake a sleeping computer, find nearby devices to pair, keep the computer awake while copying, and Windows notifications.

**Changes**

- There is no MSI package any more, because it could not update itself. If you installed from the MSI, install `NEXUS-Cloud_0.1.5_x64-setup.exe` over it once.
- NEXUS Cloud is now source-available under the Functional Source License (FSL-1.1-ALv2).

Coming from 0.1.2, 0.1.3 or 0.1.4? Install this by hand on one computer: it then passes the update on to all your other devices. From 0.1.5 on, every device also finds updates here by itself.
