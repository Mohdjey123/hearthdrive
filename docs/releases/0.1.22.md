A terminal on your other devices, from a computer or your phone, with Termux on phones.

**Terminal (Windows and Android)**

- **Open a terminal on another device.** In Devices, tap Terminal on a computer or phone that is online. Computers open PowerShell; type a line and press Enter, and it runs there. The folder you are in carries over from one line to the next, errors show in red, and ↑ and ↓ bring back earlier lines. Stop ends it and whatever it is running.
- **Termux on phones.** In Settings › Terminal on the phone, choose Termux for a full Linux shell with packages. Copy the setup command into Termux once, then check the connection. Without it, phones open Android's basic shell.
- **Only for you.** Only the owner can open a terminal, from a device with the Editor role (the role devices are paired with) or higher, and not from devices limited to some storage. Each device can keep terminals out in Settings › Terminal, which only someone on that device can change; turning it off closes the ones open. Opening a terminal is recorded in the device's activity.
- **Commands has a new icon.** The terminal icon now belongs to Terminal.

Programs that draw a full screen (like vim) or ask for a password don't work in the terminal yet.

Update both ends: the device you use and the device you open a terminal on. Computers install updates by themselves once their HearthDrive window is closed.
