Your computers' storage on the phone, Documents from other devices, and progress bars.

**New on Android**

- **Overview, Storage and Drive health.** They're now in More on the phone and show your computers: how full each one is, its folders and drives, and the health of its disks.
- **Find duplicates from the phone.** Open Storage, pick a computer and tap Find duplicates. Tick the copies you don't need and move them to the trash; one copy of each file is always kept. Adding, removing or rescanning storage, and repairing files, still happen on the computer itself.

**Fixes**

- **Documents on another device.** Opening the Documents page for another device showed "This request cannot be sent to another device". It works now, on the phone and on computers.

**Everywhere**

- **Progress bars for long actions.** Moving many files to the trash, copying, sending to other devices, checking drives and running Automation rules now show how far along they are; the longer ones can be stopped.

Update both ends: the device you use and the computer you look at. Computers install updates by themselves once their HearthDrive window is closed.
