A fix for the Devices page.

**Fixes**

- **Devices.** A paired device's buttons (Browse files, Commands, Block, Remove) no longer spill out of its card. Cards are wider, so the fingerprint and connection fit on one line, and the full address shows when you point at it. In a narrow window the buttons move onto a second line.

Coming from 0.1.4 or earlier? Install this by hand on one computer: it then passes the update on to all your other devices. From 0.1.5 on, every device finds updates here by itself.

| --- | --- |

| `HearthDrive_0.1.7_arm64.apk` | Android phones (64-bit ARM) |
