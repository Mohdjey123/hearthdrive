Open your files in other apps on Android, share them, and jump straight in from the home screen.

**New on Android**

- **Open in another app.** Files and the file preview have a new button that opens the file in another app on the phone, through Android's "Open with" list: a PDF reader, a video player, an editor.
- **Share.** Select one or more files (up to 20) and share them through Android's share menu, to a messaging app, email or anywhere else. HearthDrive fetches them from your computer first; the copies on the phone are cleared after a day.
- **Home-screen shortcuts.** Press and hold the HearthDrive icon for Back up now, Scan a document, Files and Photos. Drag one onto the home screen to keep it there.

Nothing changes on computers in this version.
