See and browse your phone's own files, on the phone and from your computers.

**New on Android**

- **This phone in Files and Photos.** Choose "This phone" in Files or Photos and tap Show this phone's files. Android asks you once to allow "All files access". After that, the phone's storage (Downloads, DCIM, Documents and the rest) shows in Files, and its photos and videos in Photos.
- **Browse the phone from your computers.** Once the phone's storage is shown, your paired computers see it in Files like any other device, while HearthDrive runs on the phone.
- **Keep it to the phone.** In Settings → Phone storage, turn off "Let my computers browse this phone" to keep the phone's files on the phone. You can also stop showing them there.

HearthDrive reads the phone's storage slowly in the background the first time, so search and photos fill in over a few minutes.
