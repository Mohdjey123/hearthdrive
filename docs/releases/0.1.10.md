A cleaner bottom bar on Android.

**Android**

- **Fewer tabs.** The bottom bar has four: Files, Photos, Devices and More. More opens a sheet with Media, Backup, Sharing, Transfers and Settings. A dot on More means transfers are running; the sheet shows how many.
- **The bar stays put.** Scrolling to the top or bottom of a page no longer pulls the whole screen, bottom bar included.

Coming from 0.1.8 on Android? 0.1.8 can't open, so install this by hand: download `HearthDrive_0.1.10_arm64.apk` on the phone and open it. From 0.1.9 on, phones offer updates themselves.

| File | For |
| --- | --- |
| `HearthDrive_0.1.10_x64-setup.exe` | Windows 10/11, 64-bit |
| `HearthDrive_0.1.10_arm64.apk` | Android phones (64-bit ARM) |
