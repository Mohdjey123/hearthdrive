Photos, music and films from your other devices.

**Fixes**

- **Photos on another device.** Opening Photos or Media for another computer, for example from your phone, showed "Not available to other devices". It now shows that computer's photos, albums, music and films, limited to the storage that device may see, as with files.

Update the computer that holds the photos: the fix runs there. Computers install updates by themselves once their HearthDrive window is closed.

Coming from 0.1.8 on Android? 0.1.8 can't open, so install this by hand: download `HearthDrive_0.1.12_arm64.apk` on the phone and open it. From 0.1.9 on, phones offer updates themselves.

| File | For |
| --- | --- |
| `HearthDrive_0.1.12_x64-setup.exe` | Windows 10/11, 64-bit |
| `HearthDrive_0.1.12_arm64.apk` | Android phones (64-bit ARM) |
